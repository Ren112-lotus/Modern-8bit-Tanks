# Historical vehicles and Japan

The outlines in `expanded_vehicle_art.py` are drawn in the game's pixel scale. The tree links represent game progression, including post-war commissioning and imported vehicles. The stats and crew represented by the damage system are game values.

The [55 expansion plates](Expansion) are photo-based studio reconstructions. [Sources and subject descriptions](Expansion/sources.json) record the photographs used to create each plate. The layouts pair an overhead and side view (nose to the right) with front and rear elevations. The sprite generator reconciles the sampled profiles with the known running gear and fittings for each variant. The Chi-Ri plate depicts an interpretation of the prototype's intended complete exterior.

The expansion sprite generator uses [vehicle-specific hull and turret sections](../expansion_vehicle_shapes.py) for the armor silhouettes and [separate exterior fittings](../expansion_vehicle_fittings.py) for running gear, roof openings and stowage. The four-view transfer supplies restrained shading, with the fittings drawn in the game grid so they remain legible when the vehicle rotates.

The [German sculpts](../german_expansion_art.py) use individual sections for the upper hull and turret. The Panzer III and StuG share their lower chassis, as do the Panther and Jagdpanther. Roof access plates, mantlets, rear exhaust housings and wheel rows were checked against the [StuG III](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/stug-iii/), [Panther](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/panther/), [Jagdpanther](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/jagdpanther/), [Tiger 131 restoration](https://tankmuseum.org/article/tiger-131-restoration-turret-part-iii), [Jagdpanzer Kanone](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/jagdpanzer-kanone), and [Bundeswehr M41](https://zms.bundeswehr.de/de/zmsbw-kanal-forschung-und-bildung/museums-und-sammlungsverbund-der-bundeswehr/militaergeschichtliche-sammlung-logistikbataillon-461-5495634) references. The longer six-wheel Ru 251 chassis follows the [prototype photograph and its captioned history](https://pzaufkl.de/spaehpanzer-ru-251-prototyp/).

The Tiger I uses the [Tiger 131 four-view reconstruction](Expansion/de_tiger_i.png) and the museum's [hull restoration photographs](https://tankmuseum.org/article/hull-tiger-131). The curved turret sides and rear follow the overhead outline. The hull deck and turret ring are set against the side elevation and the track height in the common game grid.

The Panzer IV Ausf. F2 uses the existing [sand-painted four-view reconstruction](Expansion/de_pz4f2.png). The hull steps down behind the turret to paired engine grilles. The turret carries the rear bin and single-baffle muzzle brake. The front spare links and the shape of the brake follow the [wartime front-quarter photograph](https://www.materielsterrestres39-45.fr/fr/images/charsaxe/charaxealemagneproto/pz4/pz4ausF2A/1.jpg). The [sprite comparison](panzer-iv-f2-comparison.png) shows both versions at the same scale.

The [dimensioned hull traces](../expanded_vehicle_art.py) use the same game pixel scale as the rest of the fleet. The tracing includes the A7V roof and front mount, the short three-wheel Wiesel chassis, the low ASU-57 compartment and the Ka-Mi's fitted pontoons. The Wiesel variants use their [MK plate](Fleet/de_wiesel_mk.jpg) and [TOW plate](Fleet/de_wiesel_tow.jpg). Reference dimensions and silhouettes come from the [Queensland Museum](https://www.museum.qld.gov.au/learn-and-discover/queensland-stories/mephisto), [Bundeswehr](https://www.bundeswehr.de/de/ausruestung-technik-bundeswehr/landsysteme-bundeswehr/waffentraeger-wiesel-1), [US Army vehicle tables](https://www.trngcmd.marines.mil/Portals/207/Docs/MCIS/ITEP/RITC-East/FM%20100-2-3.pdf) and [Australian War Memorial photographs](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C76272).

The [Type 89 I-Go plate](type-89-igo-four-views.png) and [Type 10 plate](type-10-four-views.png) were produced earlier from the JGSDF [I-Go archival photo](https://www.mod.go.jp/gsdf/nae/7d/siryoukan/tk_history/tk/89kou.png) and [Type 10 photo](https://www.mod.go.jp/gsdf/assets/images/equipment/ve/pic03_modal.png).

The US [M24 Chaffee](Expansion/us_m24.png) and [M41 Walker Bulldog](Expansion/us_m41.png) use the existing four-view plates for paint and panel shading. The [M24 photographs](https://www.afvdatabase.com/usa/pics/m24chaffee/m24chaffee.html) informed the rounded shield, paired front hatches and aft machine-gun mount. The [early M41 photographs](https://afvdatabase.com/usa/pics/m41bulldog/m41bulldog.html) informed the sloped turret cheeks, rear fender exhausts and cast muzzle brake. The [before-and-after comparison](chaffee-bulldog-comparison.png) shows both US vehicles at the same pixel scale.

The [JGSDF M24 plate](Expansion/jp_m24.png) and [Bundeswehr M41 plate](Expansion/de_m41g.png) supply the four-view lighting and proportions for the service variants. The M24 depicts the grey-olive Higashi-Chitose display vehicle with its rear turret bin and bare machine-gun mount. The M41 uses an olive finish, outlined turret crosses and a camouflage net packed into the rear rack as seen in Schulte's photograph. The colours are visual approximations under game lighting. [Japanese service vehicle photographs](https://aobamil.sakura.ne.jp/shasin/M24/M24.html) · [German service vehicle photographs](https://www.panzerbaer.de/types/bw_lkpz_m41-a.htm) · [Sprite comparison](jgsdf-m24-bundeswehr-m41-comparison.png)

Both chassis use a lower fender and deck, with one fewer layer in the turret body. The US and export versions of each tank share the resulting roof height, wheel diameter and pivot. The separate pintle, storage box and basket follow their respective hull or turret when it turns.

The [Panther G](Expansion/de_panther_g.png) uses the broad pale-edged camouflage bands, partial skirt panels and transverse mantlet of the [Bovington exhibit](https://tankmuseum.org/tank-nuts/tank-collection/panther). The [Ru 251](Expansion/de_ru251.png) uses the shallow turret, six road-wheel stations and square searchlight visible in the [Munster prototype photograph](https://pzaufkl.de/spaehpanzer-ru-251-prototyp). The photo corrects the wheel count on the reconstruction. The [sprite comparison](panther-g-ru251-comparison.png) shows both models at the same scale before and after the revision.
